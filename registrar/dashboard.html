Registrar Administration Information

The Registrar is responsible for reviewing student document requests, verifying submitted information, checking clearance requirements, and approving the release of official school documents.

All requests should be reviewed carefully to ensure that student records are accurate and that the required processing steps have been completed before a document is released.

Use the administration tools to monitor student accounts, payment records, clearance status, document processing, and approved document releases.
