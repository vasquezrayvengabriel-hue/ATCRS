Clearance Review Guidelines

Clearance should be reviewed before an official school document is approved for release. Confirm that the required student records and clearance information have been properly checked.

Financial and library clearance statuses should be updated according to the student's current institutional records.

If an issue is identified, record a clear note describing the matter so that the request can be reviewed and processed appropriately.

Processing Reminders

Clearance Requirements

  • Verify that the correct student and request are being reviewed.
  • Review the student's financial clearance status.
  • Review the student's library clearance status.
  • Complete the Registrar review before approving the clearance.
  • Add notes whenever an issue requires further review.
  • Confirm that all required clearance conditions are satisfied before document release.
