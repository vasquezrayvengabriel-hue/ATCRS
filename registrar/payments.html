Payment Management Guidelines

Review payment records carefully before updating their status. Confirm that the payment reference, request information, amount, and student details correspond to the correct document request.

A payment marked as paid may proceed to the next required processing stage when the student's clearance and document requirements have also been completed.

Keep payment notes clear and relevant so that other authorized Registrar personnel can understand the processing history of a request.

Payment Information

Processing Reminders

  • Check the request ID before reviewing a payment.
  • Confirm that the student information matches the associated request.
  • Record payment references accurately.
  • Update payment status only after completing the appropriate payment review.
  • Coordinate payment status with document processing and clearance requirements.
