Policy

Policies and guidelines for using the Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System.

This Policy establishes the general rules and guidelines for the use of the Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System (ATCRS) of De La Salle John Bosco College (DLSJBC).

The policy is intended to promote accurate submissions, responsible system use, secure handling of student information, and proper processing of school document requests.

1. Student Account Policy

Students must provide accurate and complete information when creating an ATCRS account.

  • A valid email address must be provided.
  • The Student ID Number must contain exactly seven digits.
  • The student's full name must be entered correctly.
  • The educational level must be selected accurately.
  • College students must provide the appropriate course or program.
  • Students must not register using another person's identity or Student ID.

2. Student Information Policy

Information submitted through ATCRS must be accurate and correspond with the student's official school records whenever applicable.

Incorrect, incomplete, or misleading information may result in a request being delayed, returned, or placed under additional verification.

3. Document Request Policy

Students may use ATCRS to request available school documents and credentials.

Available document services may include:

  • School Background or School Records
  • Transcript of Records
  • Diploma
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Certificate of Enrollment
  • Certificate of Graduation
  • Other School Documents
  • Credential Verification

4. Request Processing Policy

A submitted request must undergo the applicable review and processing procedures before a document can be released.

The general process may include:

  1. Create an account
  2. Select a document
  3. Submit a request
  4. Complete applicable payment requirements
  5. Registrar review
  6. Clearance checking
  7. Document processing
  8. Approval and release

5. Clearance Policy

Certain requests may require clearance verification before the requested document can be released.

Requests with unresolved clearance requirements may remain on hold until the applicable requirement has been completed.

6. Payment Policy

Some document requests may require applicable processing or document fees. Students should review the displayed fee before completing a request.

In this ATCRS prototype, payment functionality may be simulated for demonstration purposes. The prototype does not represent a live banking or payment system.

Users must not enter actual credit card numbers, banking passwords, or other sensitive financial information into a prototype payment form.

7. Document Release Policy

Documents may only be released after the applicable review, clearance, payment, and approval requirements have been completed.

Students must not alter, falsify, reproduce, or misrepresent official school documents.

Digital documents containing QR codes, verification codes, or digital signatures should only be used for legitimate and authorized purposes.

8. Credential Verification Policy

Authorized third parties may use the credential verification feature to confirm the authenticity of an eligible school document or credential.

Verification services must only be used for legitimate purposes and must not be used to obtain unauthorized student information.

9. Information Protection Policy

Student information and school records should only be accessed by authorized users for legitimate academic, administrative, document-processing, or verification purposes.

Users must not intentionally disclose, modify, copy, or distribute another student's information without proper authorization.

10. Account Security Policy

Students are responsible for protecting their account credentials.

  • Do not share your password with other people.
  • Do not use another student's account.
  • Do not attempt to access restricted areas without authorization.
  • Report suspected unauthorized access to the appropriate school office.

11. Acceptable Use Policy

ATCRS must only be used for legitimate school document and credential-related purposes.

Users must not:

  • Submit false information.
  • Impersonate another student.
  • Access another person's records without authorization.
  • Attempt to bypass system security.
  • Upload malicious or harmful files.
  • Interfere with the operation of ATCRS.
  • Use school documents for fraudulent purposes.

12. System Availability Policy

ATCRS is designed to provide convenient online access to document-related services. However, continuous system availability cannot be guaranteed.

The system may temporarily become unavailable because of maintenance, technical problems, internet connectivity, or other circumstances.

13. Prototype Policy

ATCRS is currently a prototype intended to demonstrate the proposed online document-request and credential-retrieval workflow.

Some features may use simulated accounts, browser storage, sample records, simulated payments, or demonstration data.

The prototype should not be treated as a production system for storing or processing confidential student records or real financial transactions.

14. Policy Changes

These policies may be updated when necessary to reflect changes in the ATCRS system, school procedures, security requirements, or applicable institutional policies.

Users are encouraged to review this page when policies are updated.

15. Compliance

By using ATCRS, students and authorized users agree to follow these policies and the applicable rules and procedures of De La Salle John Bosco College.

Policy Acknowledgment

By creating or using an ATCRS account, you acknowledge that you have read and understood the policies described on this page.
