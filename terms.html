Terms of Use

Please read these terms carefully before using the Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System.

These Terms of Use describe the rules and conditions for using the Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System (ATCRS) of De La Salle John Bosco College (DLSJBC).

By creating an account, logging into the system, submitting a document request, or using any ATCRS service, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using ATCRS, you agree to comply with these Terms of Use and any applicable rules, procedures, and requirements established for the processing and release of school documents.

If you do not agree with these terms, you should not create an account or use the services provided through the system.

2. Student Account Registration

Students may create an account to access ATCRS services. The information submitted during registration must be complete, accurate, and up to date.

  • The registered email address must be valid.
  • The Student ID Number must contain exactly seven digits.
  • The student's full name must be entered accurately.
  • The educational level and course or program, when applicable, must be correct.
  • Students must not create an account using another person's identity or Student ID.

3. Accuracy of Student Information

Students are responsible for ensuring that all information submitted through ATCRS is accurate. Incorrect or incomplete information may result in delays in processing a request.

DLSJBC may require additional information or supporting documents when necessary to verify a student's identity and records.

4. Document Requests

ATCRS allows students to submit online requests for available school records and credentials. Available services may include:

  • School Background or School Records
  • Transcript of Records
  • Diploma
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Certificate of Enrollment
  • Certificate of Graduation
  • Other School Documents
  • Credential Verification

The availability of a particular document may depend on the student's records and the procedures of the Registrar's Office.

5. Payment and Processing Fees

Some document requests may require payment of applicable processing or document fees. Students are responsible for reviewing the applicable fee before submitting a request.

The ATCRS prototype may display a simulated payment process for demonstration purposes. A simulated payment does not represent an actual banking or payment transaction.

Students should not enter actual credit card, debit card, online banking, or other sensitive financial information into a prototype payment form.

6. Request Processing and Review

Submitting a request through ATCRS does not automatically guarantee the release of a document. Requests may be reviewed and validated by the Registrar's Office.

A request may pass through the following stages:

  1. Request submission
  2. Payment confirmation
  3. Registrar review
  4. Clearance checking
  5. Document processing
  6. Approval
  7. Document release

Processing time may vary depending on the type of document, verification requirements, clearance status, and other applicable school procedures.

7. Clearance Requirements

Certain document requests may require clearance verification before a document can be released.

If a student's request has an unresolved clearance requirement, the release of the requested document may be placed on hold until the requirement has been addressed.

8. Document Release

Approved documents may be made available through the student's ATCRS account or through another authorized release method.

Students must not alter, falsify, reproduce, or misrepresent an official school document issued through the system.

Documents containing verification features such as QR codes or digital signatures must be used only for legitimate academic, employment, or other authorized purposes.

9. Digital Credential Verification

ATCRS may provide a verification mechanism that allows authorized third parties to confirm the authenticity of a credential or document.

Verification information must only be used for legitimate purposes and must not be manipulated, copied for unauthorized use, or used to misrepresent a student's academic records.

10. Acceptable Use

Users agree to use ATCRS only for lawful and legitimate purposes.

Users must not:

  • Access another student's account without authorization.
  • Submit false or misleading information.
  • Attempt to bypass system security controls.
  • Interfere with the operation of the system.
  • Upload malicious files or harmful content.
  • Use school documents for fraudulent purposes.
  • Attempt to obtain confidential records without proper authorization.

11. Account Security

Students are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

Users should not share their password with another person. If a user believes that their account has been accessed without authorization, they should report the issue to the appropriate school office.

12. Privacy

Information submitted through ATCRS may be used for account management, document processing, identity verification, clearance checking, communication, and other legitimate school processes.

For more information about the handling of information, please review our Privacy Policy .

13. System Availability

ATCRS is intended to provide convenient online access to document-related services. However, uninterrupted availability cannot be guaranteed.

The system may temporarily become unavailable because of maintenance, technical problems, internet connectivity issues, or other circumstances.

14. Intellectual Property

The ATCRS interface, system design, content, graphics, and other materials associated with the system may be protected by applicable intellectual property laws and institutional policies.

Users may not reproduce, modify, distribute, or use protected system materials for unauthorized purposes.

15. Changes to the Terms

These Terms of Use may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the ATCRS services, procedures, security requirements, or applicable institutional policies.

Continued use of ATCRS after an updated version becomes available may constitute acceptance of the revised terms.

16. Prototype Notice

This ATCRS website is a prototype designed to demonstrate the proposed Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System workflow.

Some features, including account authentication, payment processing, notifications, document storage, and digital credential verification, may be simulated in the prototype environment.

The prototype should not be treated as a production system for processing real confidential records or real financial transactions.

17. Contact and Support

For questions regarding document requests, student records, clearance requirements, or other school-related matters, users should contact the appropriate office of De La Salle John Bosco College.

Acknowledgment

By creating and using an ATCRS Student Account, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these Terms of Use and agree to follow the applicable rules and procedures of the system.
