ABOUT ATCRS

A Digital Way to Request School Records

The Automated Transcript and Credential Retrieval System is designed to simplify the process of requesting, processing, tracking, and retrieving school documents.

Convenient

Students can submit school document requests online without beginning the process through a physical visit.

Organized

Requests, payments, clearance, processing, approval, and released documents are organized in one system.

Secure

Student and request information is handled through designated Student and Registrar areas.

DOCUMENT SERVICES

What Can Students Request?

Students can select the school document they need from the Student Portal.

School Background

Request authorized school background or school record information.

Transcript of Records

Request an official Transcript of Records.

Diploma

Submit a request for diploma-related document processing.

Good Moral Certificate

Request a Certificate of Good Moral Character.

Certificate of Enrollment

Request proof of enrollment when applicable.

Certificate of Graduation

Request graduation-related certification.

Other School Document

Request another authorized school document not listed above.

Credential Verification

Support digital credential verification through secure reference information.

REQUEST PROCESS

From Request to Document Release

Students can follow the progress of their requests through the Student Portal.

01

Create Account

Create your Student Account and provide your required information.

02

Select Document

Choose the school document you want to request.

03

Submit Request

Enter the purpose and other required request information.

04

Payment

View the applicable fee and complete the prototype payment process.

05

Registrar Review

The Registrar checks the request, clearance, and payment status.

06

Document Processing

The requested school document is prepared for release.

07

Approval

The Registrar approves the request when requirements are complete.

08

Document Release

The student can access the released document through the Student Portal.

REQUIREMENTS

Before You Submit a Request

Make sure your Student Account information is complete and accurate.

Complete Student Information

Provide your email address, complete name, 7-digit Student ID, and educational level.

Correct Level and Course

Select Kindergarten to Grade 12 or College. College students must select their applicable program.

Correct Document

Select the exact document you need before submitting your request.

Clearance and Payment

Complete applicable clearance and payment requirements before document release.
